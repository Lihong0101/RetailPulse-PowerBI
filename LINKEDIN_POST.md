# LinkedIn launch post — English (ready to customize)

I recently completed **RetailPulse**, an end-to-end Power BI portfolio project analyzing a fictional retailer across **France, Germany, Italy and Spain**.

The business question: **What happens when sales grow, but profitability doesn't keep up?**

I built a multi-page report covering revenue growth, profitability drivers, product mix and returns — from raw monthly CSV files through Power Query cleaning, a dimensional model and DAX measures, to publishing in Power BI Service.

**Three findings stood out:**

📈 In France, Jan–Oct 2026 sales revenue grew approximately **11.1% YoY**, while gross margin fell approximately **5.06 percentage points**.

↩️ Spain recorded an **8.48% unit return rate**, compared with **6.71%** across the four markets.

🔍 Returns were not evenly distributed across products: France's **Consumer Tech** category had an observed return rate of approximately **11.35%**.

On the technical side, I worked with:
- **Power Query:** combining monthly CSVs, normalizing values, removing duplicate sales lines and preserving missing-price records for transparent data quality checks.
- **DAX & modeling:** time intelligence, revenue and gross-margin measures, order-date vs return-date analysis and dynamic, market-specific insight text.
- **Report design:** bookmarks, report-page tooltips, drillthrough and market filters.
- **Power BI Service:** published report, tested France/Germany row-level security roles and a successful on-demand refresh through an on-premises gateway.

The biggest lesson: a dashboard should explain a decision, not just display numbers. These findings are descriptive signals for further investigation, not proof of causation.

**All data is synthetic; this is an independent portfolio project.**
